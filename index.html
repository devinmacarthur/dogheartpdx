The approach

No force. No fear.
Just relationship.

Everything here runs on positive reinforcement — building trust and confidence instead of punishing fear. It's slower than shortcuts, and it's the only way I work. Four words guide every visit:

Attunement

Reading each dog's body language and meeting them exactly where they are.

Empathy

Every quirk has a reason. Fear gets patience here, never punishment.

Safety

Calm handling, careful introductions, and a fully insured practice.

Enrichment

Sniffy walks, new places, games, and choices — a fuller world for your dog.
